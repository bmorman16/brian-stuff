1.	Log in to My LTP through Worklife > Browse Menu > Career > Learning Together Program > My LTP
2.	Select "Actions" from the top menu
3.	Select "Submit Grades"
4.	Select your course(s) then click "Next"
5.	Select the grade achieved from the drop down menu
Note: If you have received a GPA, use your school's conversion chart to input the letter grade equivalent. Certificates of completion = P (Pass)
6.	Upload your grade report by selecting "Choose File" or click the 'I will fax my documentation instead of uploading' check box to receive instructions for submitting via fax
7.	Click "Submit" to send your grade submission to the LTP Service Center for review
